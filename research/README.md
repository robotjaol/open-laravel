# Research dossier

Method: desk research on 2026-11-18 using web search, plus the author's engineering judgement. Each document marks claims as **[S]** sourced (URL in the document and in `source-register.md`), **[J]** professional judgement, or **[A]** assumption. Product details that were not verified in this session are labelled "not verified" instead of stated as fact.

Limits: no interviews, no usage telemetry, no hands-on benchmarking of competitors. Findings about third-party products are qualitative.
